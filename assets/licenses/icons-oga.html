Preview: 

105 game related icons included in both black and white, in 1x and 2x sizes. Also included are spritesheets and vector source files.

Arrows, audio, menu, bars, basket, buttons, shopping, checkmark, contrast, cross, exit, exclamation, import/export, video, gamepad, settings, home, information, joystick, leadboards, lock, singleplayer, multiplayer, grid, medal, mouse, power, play/pause/stop, scroll, save, star, signal, trophy, trashcan, warning and zoom.

Includes:

  • Icons in black and white, 1x and 2x size
  • Separate PNG sprites (105x)
  • Spritesheets
  • Vector files

Let me know if you have any questions or requests!
