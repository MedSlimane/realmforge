# Character model inspection previews

These transparent PNGs are **bind-pose / detached-part inspection stills** rendered from the user-supplied Nintendo NSMBU models, ripped by DogToon64. They are not idle frames or gameplay animation and are not registered in the game manifest.

Each file is rendered directly at 192×192. Standard big forms occupy at most 120px of height; small forms and detached caps occupy at most 60px (plus antialiasing). No source texture or PNG is enlarged. `side` is orthographic from the source's lateral axis; detached caps retain their source-local orientation. Source variant stems remain in filenames rather than guessing form names.

Reproduction scripts, provenance, source/output hashes, assembly details, missing-animation evidence and acquisition instructions are in `assets/packs/mario/character-model-import/MODEL_REPORT.md` in this repository. No `manifest-fragment.json` exists because no compatible original animation channels were acquired.

Credit Nintendo and DogToon64. Private class demo / inspection only; do not deploy this Nintendo pack publicly.
